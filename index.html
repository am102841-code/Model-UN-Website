01 — WHAT IS MODEL UN?

Diplomacy,
brought to life.

Model United Nations is an educational simulation where students represent countries and work together to debate international issues, negotiate solutions, and create resolutions.

02 — WHY WE MADE THIS CLUB

More than
just debate.

We created Thomas Russell Model UN to give students a place to build confidence, learn about the world, and develop skills that extend beyond the classroom.

Whether you're an experienced speaker or stepping up to a podium for the first time, our goal is to create a welcoming environment where everyone can learn, collaborate, and lead.
